# Paper foundations

Origami Lab asks what can be *constructed* from the sheet a person or Clydie is holding. A grab supplies a place, a direction and perhaps a snap. The engine supplies the material that can follow and tests the proposed swing. The working model is one zero-thickness sheet with piecewise-rigid faces and a single-axis, exterior 180° fold. [Guiding principles](PRINCIPLES.md) · [Material motion review](https://github.com/tsincla-purdue/origami-lab/blob/81dac0aca1b576c3ff310b3c10d736df5627b6da/docs/MATERIAL_MOTION_REVIEW.md)

## What the paper knows

Material points have coordinates on the original square. Each convex facet carries a rigid placement, front/back orientation from that placement, and a rank that orders plies where they overlap. A shared segment in the original sheet is a material attachment. Two outlines touching in the view provide different evidence. Splitting retains the fixed child's ID and allocates one for the moving child. A crease or mark can persist after Unfold restores the earlier mesh. Undo instead removes the last recorded action. [Paper representation](../vendor/origamio/src/paper.ts) · [Material neighbours](../vendor/origamio/src/rigid.ts) · [History](../src/lab/experiment.ts)

For a given crease and moving side, inference clips each placed facet to the moving half-plane. Only the positive-area portion of a selected parent can rotate. The ordinary grab swings toward the exposed surface of the current view. Turning the paper over reverses the visible stack. Three relations govern the search.

| Geometry at the start | Consequence for an outward grab |
| --- | --- |
| A material weld reaches into the moving half-plane | Both incident portions must share the motion. Otherwise an off-axis material point would acquire two positions. |
| An upper portion covers a lower one over positive area in the moving half-plane | A lower mover must carry the upper portion to launch through that surface while the rest stays flat. The upper ply can leave the lower one behind if its welds permit. |
| A weld lies along the axis | Its points stay fixed under rotation. It can join a larger interpretation, and supplies no forced inclusion by itself. A weld wholly on the fixed side supplies no pull. |

Reachability from the seed along the first two relations is the least set satisfying those implications. Every following edge adds a consequence of a selected portion, and propagation stops when there are no further consequences. The generator also tries the original grab, local stack-prefix closures and a larger expansion using on-axis joins. These are hypotheses about the hand. The same validator evaluates each one. Connectivity of a one-grip packet, clearance at the hinge and order at landing require further evidence. Explicit Whole stack supplies coordinated actuation of the half-plane. ## What flat-folding theory contributes

For a fixed, flat, piecewise-isometric placement with an order on overlapping faces, consider a cross-section through the **interior of a common projected segment**. A flat stretch of material passes through it. At a folded crease, two incident plies turn back on the same side. Akitaya, Demaine and Ku describe consistency conditions for such layer orders, including how to handle unfolded seams between convex faces. Their question is whether the endpoint order is compatible with the material. [Computing Flat-Folded States, §§3.1–3.2 and Figure 2](https://erikdemaine.org/papers/FlatFolder_OSME2024/paper.pdf)

| Local condition | Application and evidential force |
| --- | --- |
| Tortilla–tortilla | Two continuing flat sheets must keep the same relative order across the segment, including across a coplanar subdivision. This is a **necessary endpoint condition**. It helps identify when a clearance test depends on an incidental seam. Consistency alone supplies no launch or clearance certificate. |
| Taco–tortilla | A sheet continuing through a folded edge cannot lie between that edge's incident plies where they close. The validator's `blocked-hinge` test detects one **necessary obstruction** when an uncut stationary cover actually spans the weld and the endpoint ranks sandwich it locally. A crossing elsewhere on the projected axis would be insufficient. |
| Taco–taco | Two folded edges coinciding along an interval and turning to the same side may nest or sit apart. Alternating incident plies would interleave the folds. This is a **necessary endpoint condition** for those contacts and a useful diagnostic for a future two-hinge example. The current validator has no general folded-edge-pair test. |

The **sufficient local clearance witness** used today comes from the construction. At a freshly split hinge, the two pieces shared a parent and rank. A crossing sheet behind them at launch and behind both at landing can clear this hinge under the supported half-turn. Other checks must also pass. Carrying a cover during inference is a **candidate-generation rule**. No combination of the three endpoint observations by itself establishes a grip or a continuous path. ## What remains open

- **Equivalent ancestry can produce different certificates.** Score the right strip at $x=3/4$. Flatten it by recorded Unfold in one history and by folding the strip back in another. Fold the whole right half left at $x=1/2$, then try to lift the upper strip at $x=1/4$. Both histories have the same material placement, local stack order and crease marks. A fresh split earns `accepted / packet-fold`. The retained coplanar seam gives `unverified / hinge-cover`. Ancestry supplies the first certificate. Generalizing it would require a sufficient argument for the existing seam. [Executable regression](../tests/packets.test.ts) · [Construction IV.G](https://github.com/tsincla-purdue/origami-lab/blob/81dac0aca1b576c3ff310b3c10d736df5627b6da/docs/MATERIAL_MOTION_REVIEW.md#g-a-subdivision-changes-the-certificate-on-reachable-states)
- **Inference searches a limited family.** It can miss intermediate packet choices. A larger set passing the validator supplies no evidence that the user grabbed it. Materially distinct plausible interpretations belong in the ordinary preview and choice path.
- **Certification has a finite scope.** `accepted` means a supported constructive operation passed its checks. `rejected` records an invalid request or a witnessed model violation. `unverified` records insufficient evidence, including unresolved clearance, near-degenerate geometry and resource limits. Both refusals preserve the paper. The export audit checks endpoints and history numerically. It cannot independently certify a refusal reason, finite thickness or general continuous 3D motion. [Scope and numerical policy](SOUNDNESS.md)

## When to revisit the engine

Use the current model for exploration once the canonical constructions, counterexamples, replay, human-input and tactile suites, and independent audit are green. Reopen an engine question when an ordinary gesture or exported construction shows a reproducible unexpected carry, a missed interpretation with a material witness, an invalid accepted endpoint, or a conservative refusal for which a sufficient clearance argument can be stated. Keep the original history, identify the intended means of actuation, and test a nearby permitted and forbidden case before changing a verdict. A recurring difficulty acquiring a sound fold is evidence to examine the gesture path separately. These criteria let Clydie's experiments determine the next physical question.
