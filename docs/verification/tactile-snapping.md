# Tactile snapping walkthrough

1. Fold the square horizontally and unfold it to leave a reference crease.
   Hover over either end of that crease. Check that the ring sits exactly on
   the intersection, with both references highlighted and a layer named.
   On touch, press briefly to see the same preview. Tap and select **Mark point**.
2. Grab the lower edge away from a corner. Drag toward the mark and watch for
   **Grabbed point onto mark**, the landing ring, and the moving packet. Move
   slightly around the mark, then pull away. Hold **Alt** with a mouse to
   release snapping, or switch **Snap to points** off. Release at the mark to
   fold, then use **Undo**. This is a synthetic apex-transfer exercise. The
   user's hexagon export was unavailable in the checkout and attachments.
3. Open **Advanced Controls → Draw crease** and sketch near one mark. The
   crease should slide through the mark while preserving its direction. Mark
   the other end of the crease and sketch between the two marks to acquire their
   exact line. Tap the side to move and confirm once the preview is accepted.
4. Try step 1 again after a vertical half fold and **Flip over**. Where material
   intersections coincide or are close, use **Intersection layer** to inspect
   the alternatives. When a fold has competing targets, release to inspect
   **Snap target** and confirm the chosen preview. Pulling away or pressing
   **Escape** clears the selection in progress. Export and re-import the
   experiment to inspect the same resolved axes and material marks in history.
